IG Unfollow Tracker

    Import your Instagram data

    This reads the file Instagram gives you when you export your own data. It sends nothing to Instagram, so it can't trigger rate limits or warnings on your account.

    How to get the file (about 2 minutes to request)
    1. On instagram.com: More → Settings → Meta Account → Your information and permissions → Export your information.
    2. Create export → pick your profile → Export to device.
    3. Customize information → tick only Followers and following.
    4. Date range: All time. Format: JSON (not HTML). Then Start export.
    5. Instagram emails you when it's ready — for just followers and following that's usually minutes, though Instagram says it can take longer. Download the .zip (available for 4 days).

    Menu names change now and then; the “Export your information” tool is the part to look for. The export shows your lists as of the moment Instagram prepared it.
